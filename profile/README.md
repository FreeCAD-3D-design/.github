# FreeCAD - Parametric 3D Modeling for Engineering and Design

## About FreeCAD

FreeCAD is an open-source parametric 3D modeler built for mechanical engineering, industrial design, architecture, and related technical fields. FreeCAD parametric modeling lets users sketch, constrain, and rebuild geometry through a history-based workflow, so changes stay controlled instead of destructive. With FreeCAD 3D design tools, a single model can move from concept to solid, then into assemblies, drawings, and documentation. FreeCAD CAD sessions stay readable for beginners and deep enough for experienced engineers who need precise dimensions and repeatable results. Because FreeCAD open source development is community-driven, the platform keeps growing through workbenches that cover part design, drafting, simulation, and fabrication preparation.

## Overview

FreeCAD turns parametric intent into measurable geometry. FreeCAD CAD users begin with constrained sketches, then build features that remain linked to their source dimensions. That structure matters in FreeCAD mechanical design, where a single change to a hole diameter or plate thickness should update the entire part and its drawing. FreeCAD 3D design also reaches beyond machinery: architectural elements, custom fixtures, and product concepts all benefit from the same parametric discipline. FreeCAD open source development means workbenches expand over time, covering drafting, FEM, CAM, and sheet metal tasks. For anyone comparing CAD options, FreeCAD parametric modeling offers a serious open path from first sketch to finished documentation.

## Key Features of FreeCAD

- **Parametric sketches** – FreeCAD parametric modeling starts with constrained 2D geometry that drives 3D features.
- **Solid and surface tools** – FreeCAD 3D design supports pads, pockets, revolutions, lofts, and sweeps for complex shapes.
- **Assembly structure** – FreeCAD mechanical design organizes parts and subassemblies with positional relationships.
- **Technical drawings** – FreeCAD CAD produces views, dimensions, and annotations from the same model.
- **Workbench depth** – FreeCAD open source modules extend into FEM, CAM, BIM, and spreadsheet-driven parameters.
- **History-based edits** – Change an early sketch and downstream features rebuild predictably.
